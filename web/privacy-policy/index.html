WHY IS LOCATION COLLECTED? HOW IS IT USED?

We use Google Route Optimization API which is a routing algorithm for generating pickup & delivery routes for last mile delivery to the customers. These routes are then downloaded on our delivery app to do the deliveries and Google Route Optimization API on the phone used by riders helps us track the location of the rider. Locus needs to know the path being followed by the rider. Location information of the rider will be used to send relevant updates to the end customer.

HOW ARE YOUR LOCATION DETAILS COLLECTED?

Your location details are collected using a third-party library or google play services based on configurations.

CONSUMER APP

Data retention

SWACHHAM retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain customer accounts, process orders, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements.

Customer account information, order history, transaction records, payment records, and operational data are retained while the account remains active and for such additional period as may be regulatory requirements, taxation obligation, audit requirements, taxation obligation, audit requirements, and legitimate business purposes.

When personal information is no longer required for these purposes, it will be permanently deleted, anonymized, or securely destroyed using industry-standard security practices.

Data deletion

Customers may request deletion of their personal information and account data at any time by contacting our support team at info@swachham.co.in

Upon receiving and verifying a valid deletion request, SWACHHAM. will delete or anonymize the customer's personal information from its active systems.

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law, regulatory obligations, taxation requirements, audit requirements, fraud prevention purposes, dispute resolution processes, or other legitimate legal obligations. Such information will be retained only for the period required and will be securely deleted thereafter.

For account deletion or personal data deletion requests, please contact:

Email: info@swachham.co.in

You can also delete your account yourself, without contacting us. In the app, open Profile and choose "Delete account". If you no longer have the app installed, use the account deletion page on our website; it asks only for your registered mobile number.

Both routes confirm the request before anything is deleted. The website route sends a one-time code to the registered mobile number, and the deletion is carried out only after that code is entered, so that an account cannot be deleted by someone who merely knows the number.

When the account is deleted, your name, email address, mobile number, password, profile photograph, saved addresses, cart, notifications and reviews are removed. If you have never placed an order, the account record itself is removed and nothing is kept.

If you have placed orders, the records of those orders are kept, because we are required to retain billing and taxation records. Those records are detached from your identity: the personal details on the account are erased, and what remains does not name you.
